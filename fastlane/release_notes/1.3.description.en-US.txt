Open Lore and see the places around you. Tap a pin, find out what stands on the corner, and get walking directions. Use your location to explore nearby or pick a city and browse from home.

THE MAP
Explore landmarks across U.S. cities. Each included place has a source you can follow. Look for book-shaped pins to find a story you can read or listen to in every included city. Save places on your device for your next walk.

OUT WALKING
Use the nearby finder to see which landmarks are within range and which direction to look. In Chicago, Seattle and Washington, D.C., follow an included self-guided walk from stop to stop. Lore remembers where you left off.

ON YOUR DEVICE
The included landmarks, stories and walks work without an account or subscription. Street-map tiles, external sources and directions may need a connection. Location and camera access are optional. Coverage varies by location.

LORE+
When Lore’s online services are available, eligible users can purchase additional features. Apple shows the price before purchase. Use Account to restore purchases or manage your subscription.
