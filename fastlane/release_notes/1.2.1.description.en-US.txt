Discover the history of places around you with Lore. The free Starter Guide is included in the app, so you can explore its cities and landmarks even when Lore’s online catalog is unavailable.

IN THE STARTER GUIDE
Browse by city or search for a landmark. Read its story, save it on this device, and follow its source or open walking directions in Apple Maps when you have a connection. No sign-in or purchase is needed to read the included guide.

WHEN ONLINE SERVICES ARE AVAILABLE
Explore Lore’s wider map, point the camera at catalogued landmarks, and follow city tours. Signed-in visitors can keep a private visit journal with notes and photos. Coverage varies by location. Distances and scanner placements are estimates.

LORE+
Lore offers monthly and annual subscriptions and an optional lifetime purchase when its online services are available. Apple confirms prices and eligibility before payment. Use Account to restore purchases or manage your subscription.

PRIVACY
The Starter Guide can be explored without camera or location access. When using the live scanner, camera and location help place nearby landmarks. Optional cloud landmark identification asks before sending a still image.
