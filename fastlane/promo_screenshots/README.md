# Lore 1.2.1 screenshots

These native simulator captures show the Starter Guide bundled with Lore 1.2.1 build 50. The three screens on each device show the city list, the four Chicago landmarks, and Cloud Gate’s story with its source, walking directions and save control. The offline journey also checks search and access to Restore purchases.

Release source `1ea19ad3068ae0dde3c102b4226818f2af49896f` produced the iPhone 17 Pro Max (1320 × 2868) and iPad Pro 13-inch M5 (2064 × 2752) screenshots. The original opaque RGB PNGs are in `en-US`. [CAPTURE.json](CAPTURE.json) records their byte lengths, dimensions, SHA-256 hashes, simulator runtime and capture times.
